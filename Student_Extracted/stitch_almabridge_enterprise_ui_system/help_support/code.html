How do I connect with a mentor? expand_more

To connect with a mentor, navigate to the Mentorship tab in the sidebar. You can browse through our list of verified alumni and industry professionals. Simply click on a mentor's profile and hit the "Request Connection" button. You'll be notified once they accept your invitation.

Is the AI Career Helper free for students? expand_more

Yes, the fundamental AI features, including resume scanning and basic interview practice, are completely free for all registered student accounts. Premium features like personalized industry mapping and deep-dive technical interview simulations may require an institutional license or a premium subscription.

Can I change my internship preferences? expand_more

Absolutely. Go to Jobs & Internships and click on the 'Preferences' gear icon in the top right. Here you can update your preferred industries, locations, and roles. The AI recommendation engine will update your dashboard feed instantly.

How do I access university-exclusive events? expand_more

University-exclusive events are automatically visible on your Events dashboard based on your registered academic email domain. Look for the "University Verified" badge on event cards to identify exclusive opportunities.

Can't find what you're looking for?

Our technical support engineers are standing by to assist you with any platform issues or account inquiries. Most tickets are resolved within 24 hours.
